Explanation of each research

Computer Technology:
Each level of computer technology increases the maximum number of fleets by one. 

Combustion Drive:
Allow the construction of Small Cargo and Large Cargo ships, Light Fighters, Recyclers and Crawlers. In addition, all ships that use the Combustion Drive will have their Base Speed increased by 10% for every level of the Combustion Drive technology.

Laser Technology:
Laser technology allows research into the laser based weapon systems used by a number of defensive structures and ships. This technology is also a necessity for expansion into ion and plasma based weapon systems.

Armour Technology:
Increases hull plating strength of ships and defenses by +10% of base value per level.

Impulse Drive:
Advanced propulsion enabling Heavy Fighters, Cruisers, Colony Ships, Bombers. +20% base speed per level for ships using this drive.

Astrophysics:
Allows colonizing more planets and sending more expeditions. Every 2 levels grants +1 colony slot.

Stealth Systems:
Advanced cloaking and electronic warfare capabilities for covert operations.

Ion Technology:
Enables concentrated ion beams for damage.

Plasma Technology:
Advanced high-energy plasma weaponry.

Weapon Technology:
Increases weapon strength of ships and defenses by +10% of base value per level.

Shielding Technology:
Increases shield efficiency of ships and defenses by +10% of base value per level.

Hyperspace Technology:
Enables research into larger ship propulsion and faster travel. +5% cargo capacity per level.

Hyperspace Drive:
Top-tier propulsion for Battleships, Destroyers, etc. +30% base speed per level for ships using this drive.
