This document is here to showcase the different ship types and a short description of what they do/are.

---

Transport ships:

Small Cargo Ship: A nimble vessel designed for hauling resources between planets. Ideal for quick raids and small-scale resource transfers.

Large Cargo Ship: An upgraded hauler with significantly more storage capacity than its smaller counterpart. Best suited for moving large quantities of resources after major operations.

---

Fighter type crafts:

Light Fighter: A lightweight combat vessel found in nearly every fleet. Cheap to produce but limited in both defensive capability and cargo space.

Heavy Fighter: A more robust successor to the Light Fighter, featuring improved shields and greater firepower at a higher production cost.

Bomber: A specialized attack craft engineered to punch through heavily fortified planetary defenses.

---

Line ships:

Cruiser: A mid-tier warship boasting nearly triple the armor of a Heavy Fighter and over twice the offensive output. Also among the fastest ships in any fleet.

Battleship: The cornerstone of large-scale military operations. Combines heavy plating, powerful weapons, high cruise speed, and a generous cargo hold into a formidable all-around combat vessel.

Battlecruiser: A fleet-killer specializing in engagements against capital ships. Its advanced laser systems allow it to tear through heavy vessels with remarkable efficiency.

Destroyer: The most massive warship available, equipped with unmatched destructive capability.

---

Special ships:

Recycler: A utility ship designed to gather and reclaim valuable materials from orbital debris fields.

Colony Ship: A self-sufficient vessel equipped with everything needed to establish a settlement on an uncharted world.

Crawler: A surface-based mining unit that boosts Titanium, Helium3, and DarkMatter output on its assigned planet by 0.02% each.
