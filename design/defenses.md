This document is here to showcase the different defense structures and a short description of what they do/are.

---

Light Defenses:

Rocket Launcher: A simple but cost-effective ballistic emplacement that provides basic deterrence against lightly armed raiders.

Light Laser: A focused energy weapon offering slightly better firepower than the Rocket Launcher while remaining inexpensive to mass-produce.

---

Heavy Defenses:

Heavy Laser: An upscaled laser battery delivering significantly more damage per shot, capable of threatening mid-tier warships.

Ion Cannon: A specialized beam weapon with an exceptionally powerful shield for its class, able to endure sustained bombardment while returning steady fire.

Gauss Cannon: A magnetically accelerated kinetic weapon dealing devastating single-shot damage, rivaling the firepower of capital ships.

Plasma Turret: The ultimate planetary weapon platform, unleashing superheated plasma bolts that can cripple even the most heavily armored vessels.

---

Shield Domes:

Small Shield Dome: A planetary energy barrier that absorbs incoming fire across all defensive positions. Limited to one per planet.

Large Shield Dome: A vastly reinforced shield generator providing five times the coverage of its smaller counterpart. Limited to one per planet.
